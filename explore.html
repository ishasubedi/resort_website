Your base in Mustang

Lete sits on the Beni–Jomsom road, with some of Nepal's best-known places a drive away. It is also a handy stop for self-driving visitors, a growing group in Mustang.

Dhaulagiri, Nilgiri and Annapurna I

The valley runs between Dhaulagiri (8,167 m) and Annapurna I (8,091 m), with Nilgiri close by. Lete and neighbouring Kalopani are among the few places on the Circuit with a view of Annapurna I.

Kalopani and Ghasa

Next-door villages on the same road. Kalopani lies just north of Lete and the two have grown into one settlement. Ghasa is to the south.

Annapurna Circuit

One of the world's best-known trekking routes passes through this region.

Upper Mustang and Lo Manthang

The old walled kingdom in the north. Upper Mustang is a restricted area that needs a special permit, so arrange it through a trekking agency.

Muktinath

A famous temple and pilgrimage site, roughly 20 km from Jomsom by road.

Kagbeni

A village where the Kali Gandaki meets another river, on the road towards Lo Manthang.

Jomsom

The district centre of Lower Mustang, with the airport and a long bazaar.

Marpha

A neat village in the Thak Khola valley, known for its apple orchards.

Tatopani

South of Lete on the same valley road, known for its natural hot springs. It is roughly 25 to 30 km by road.

Kali Gandaki Gorge

Often described as one of the deepest gorges in the world, with Dhaulagiri and Annapurna above it.

Know before you go

Lete is at roughly 2,400 to 2,500 metres. Pack warm layers for the evenings in every season. If you continue to Muktinath (around 3,800 m), allow time to adjust. Ask the resort about payment methods and mobile coverage before you travel.

When to come

Spring (March to May)Popular for trekking and clear mountain views.
Monsoon (June to August)Mustang sits in the rain shadow, so it stays drier than most of Nepal, but landslides can close the road.
Autumn (mid-September to mid-November)The time Nepal tourism information recommends for Mustang.
Winter (December to February)Cold, with snow on the peaks. Ask the resort about conditions before you come.
