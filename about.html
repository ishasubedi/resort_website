The story behind the stay

About the resort

The place

Lete is a village on the Beni–Jomsom road in Mustang, in the lower Kali Gandaki valley, at roughly 2,400 to 2,500 metres. The resort stands in Thasang-3, with a stone-clad main building and A-frame cottages among the pines.

The owner

Karan KC, a fast bowler for Nepal's national cricket team, trekked in the Himalayas for years and kept imagining a home among the mountains. The resort is that idea opened to travellers, as a small contribution to Nepal's tourism. It has a main building and several standalone cottages.
