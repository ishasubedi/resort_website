Privacy

What this website stores

This website does not use cookies or analytics, and it does not save anything about you in your browser.

The booking form

What you type (name, phone, email, dates, guests, notes) is used only to prepare your request. You send it to the resort yourself by WhatsApp or email. If the resort connects an online form service, your request goes to that service and to the resort instead. The resort uses these details only to answer your request.

Third parties

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads. WhatsApp and your email provider handle your message under their own policies once you send it. The Google Maps link opens Google Maps in a new tab.

Questions or removal

To ask what the resort holds about you or to have it deleted, contact theletegaunresort@gmail.com.
